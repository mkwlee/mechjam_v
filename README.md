# Operation: Circuit Meltdown

*A Metroidvania-inspired mech action game developed for MechJam V*

“**At ease soldier.** We’ve received intel on a strategically vital robot compound with a reactor in critical condition. Find the scattered isotopes to trigger a meltdown—the ultimate assault mission. Once that last isotope is in, run like hell.”

🔗 [Play on itch.io](https://matthewtofu.itch.io/operation-circuit-meltdown)

---

## 🎮 Game Overview

**Operation: Circuit Meltdown** puts you in control of a high-tech mech exploring a facility full of robotic threats. Inspired by classic Metroidvania design, the game encourages exploration, power discovery, and smart use of tools in an interconnected map.

---

## 🕹️ Controls & Mechanics

- **Death is not the end**: You keep all collected isotopes and items after respawning.
- **Enemy respawn farming**: Walk slightly off-screen to reset enemies and farm health.
- **Environmental hazards**: Water damages you but can be crossed if you're quick.
- **No contact damage**: Enemies like chargers can be used as moving platforms.

---

## 🤖 Enemies

- **Charger**: Moves left/right only—can be used to platform.
- **Dropper**: Drops materials while moving—time your approach.
- **Exploder**: Fragile, but explodes if you stay in its line of sight.

---

## 🔫 Weapons

- **The Gun**: Standard rapid-fire blaster.
- **Electric Gun**: High burst damage with a short charge-up.
- **Fire Gun**: Flamethrower-style damage-over-time weapon.

---

## 🧩 Other Gameplay Elements

- **Water**: Harms you, but sometimes necessary to cross.
- **Healing**: Gained by destroying enemies and collecting isotopes.
- **Doors**: Some require specific weapons to destroy and open.

---
